Dive into the **System Design Sandbox**—a collaborative playground where I tackle real-world system design challenges to master high-level design (HLD)! Whether you're a beginner or a pro, this open-source repo is your space to learn, design, and contribute to scalable architectures like URL Shorteners and beyond. 🌐

## Why Join the Sandbox? 🎯
As a senior engineer, I’m passionate about breaking down complex systems into clear, scalable designs. This repo is my learning lab and your resource for:
- Exploring practical HLD problems with detailed solutions.
- Following my design journey through iterations and trade-offs.
- Contributing your own ideas to shape the future of system design!

## Get Involved! 🌟
- **Star** this repo to show some love! 💖
- **Fork** it to experiment with your own designs.
- **Contribute** by:
  - Suggesting a problem (e.g., “Design a chat app”) via our [fun issue template](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/suggest-system-design.yml).
  - Improving a design with a PR.
  - Adding a resource or badge idea.
- Check [contribute.md](contribute.md) for easy ways to jump in, even if it’s your first PR! 🌱
